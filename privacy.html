The game

Little Keepings collects no data. Progress and settings are stored only on your device. The game contains no advertising or tracking.

In-app purchase on Android

On Android, the first chapter is free and a one-time purchase unlocks the rest. Google Play (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) handles the purchase and payment under its own terms; we do not receive your payment details. The game connects to Google Play only to show the price, complete a purchase and check whether it is valid, and it stores the result on your device. We receive purchase and order information from Google as far as needed for accounting and refunds (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). See the Google Privacy Policy.
