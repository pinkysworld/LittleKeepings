A sorting puzzle with no timer

Sorting should calm you down, not rush you. Here is how to spot a game that gives you time.

Why time pressure spoils sorting

Many sorting and tidying games count down seconds or reward speed. If you play to unwind, that quickly feels like stress. Without a clock you can try ideas, rethink a move and focus on the pattern.

What to look for

  • Is there a timer or a time bonus? The store description usually says so.
  • Can you take moves back without a penalty?
  • Are the rules visible, or do you have to guess them?
  • Do ads interrupt play?

How Little Keepings handles it

Little Keepings has no timer. The rules of each task are visible, and Undo takes back a move at any time. There are 25 tasks in five chapters, from buttons and pens to keepsakes with matching threads. You play with mouse or touch on Windows 10 or newer.

FAQ

Does Little Keepings have a timer?

No. There is no timer; you play at your own pace.

Can I undo moves?

Yes, Undo takes back a move at any time.

How many tasks are there?

25 tasks in five chapters.
