A sorting game for Windows without ads

A puzzle to unwind with, with no ads cutting in. Here is what to check in the store.

Why ads spoil relaxing

In calm puzzle and sorting games, pop-ups break your concentration. Before downloading, check the store page: it lists notes on ads and purchases, the age rating and privacy details.

What to look for

  • Notes on ads or in-game purchases in the store description
  • Data collection statements in the privacy policy
  • Whether progress is stored on your device

Little Keepings

According to our privacy policy, Little Keepings contains no ads and no tracking. The game collects no data; progress and settings stay on your device. It runs on Windows 10 or newer, with 25 tasks in five chapters, no timer and an Undo. See the Microsoft Store for the age rating.

FAQ

Does Little Keepings show ads?

No. Per the privacy policy, the game has no ads and no tracking.

Where is my progress saved?

Locally on your device.

Which system does it run on?

Windows 10 or newer, available in the Microsoft Store.
