Little Keepings

A tidying game to relax with

Putting small things in order without leaving your chair: why tidying games can feel calming.

Why tidying games appeal

Ordering has a clear goal and a visible result: first a mess, then a fit. That can feel good at the end of a long day. What matters is that the game does not create pressure of its own.

What to look for

  • A calm pace instead of a clock
  • Clear rules
  • The option to take mistakes back

Little Keepings

In Little Keepings you arrange small finds: buttons, pens, spice tins, tools and keepsakes with matching threads. Each task shows its rules. There is no timer, and Undo takes back a move. Play with mouse or touch on Windows 10 or newer.

FAQ

What do you sort in Little Keepings?

Small finds such as buttons, pens, spice tins, tools and keepsakes.

Do I need experience?

No, the rules of each task are shown.

Can I play with touch?

Yes, mouse and touch are supported.
